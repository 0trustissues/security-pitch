Security · Agentic SOC

You can't bolt on an agentic SOC.

Meeting adversaries at machine speed requires a platform where data, AI and automation are native.

Legacy SOC (Human-in-the-loop)

Slow, manual process. The analyst is the bottleneck. Disconnected tools create friction.

Agentic SOC (Human-on-the-loop)

Machine speed with human judgement. The platform builds, the analyst approves.

Theoretical autonomous SOC

No human oversight. Risky and irresponsible. Not for security teams.
